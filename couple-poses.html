    One flash, one umbrella

    • Manual everything. Flash on a stand in manual power; set aperture for depth, then fix brightness with flash power, not the aperture.
    • Closer is softer. Bring the umbrella as close as the framing allows. Pull it back only to cover a group evenly.
    • Shoot-through or reflective. Shoot-through spreads wider and suits groups; reflective gives more shape. Positions in this book work for both.
    Settings are starting points for open shade or indoor light; adjust flash power first. Above 1/250 s you need high-speed sync, which costs about two stops of flash power.
